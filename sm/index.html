Core operational background

Ship management breaks down when maintenance, inventory, and superintendent oversight live in disconnected systems — especially across satellite gaps and multi-vessel fleets.

Administrative drag at sea

Engineers and officers re-enter the same job data across PMS, SPICS, permits, and defect sheets instead of executing work.

Connectivity as a bottleneck

Cloud-only workflows stall when VSAT drops — delaying confirmations, stock truth, and HQ visibility.

Fragmented fleet picture

Superintendents reconcile emailed Excel packs instead of a single exportable record from report → confirm → approve.

Procurement friction

Requisitions and RFQs often restart from scratch because vessel ROB and consumption history are not tied to maintenance events.
